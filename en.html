🤔 What is Animal Face Test?

This service is an AI tool that analyzes facial features using Google's Teachable Machine technology to find the animal you look like most. The AI, trained on thousands of animal face data, analyzes your impression.

How to use?

1. Click 'Start Webcam' to analyze in real-time.
2. Upload a photo of your face for analysis.

※ All images are processed locally in your browser and never sent to a server. Your privacy is protected!
